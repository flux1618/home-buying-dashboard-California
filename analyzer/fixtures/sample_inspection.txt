1. GENERAL

3. HVAC
HVAC: central split system, data plate indicates the unit is 16 years old. Cooling
differential measured at 14 degrees. Service records were not available.

4. PLUMBING
Water heater: 40 gallon gas, 11 years old.
Water source: public water supply through LADWP.
Sewer: public sewer connection serving the street. The lateral was not scoped and its
condition could not be determined.

5. SITE
A recorded utility easement crosses the rear 15 feet of the parcel.
The rear yard shows evidence of past standing water near the fence line; the seller
disclosed water intrusion in the crawl space in 2019, since remediated.
The detached workshop appears to have been built without a permit on file with the city.

6. COVENANTS
The subdivision is governed by an HOA. Dues of $85.00 per month are payable to the
association per the recorded covenants. A special assessment for roof resurfacing was discussed
at the 2025 annual meeting but has not been levied.

7. SUMMARY OF DEFICIENCIES
- The crawl space vapor barrier is displaced and should be repaired.
- GFCI protection is absent at the kitchen counter receptacles, a safety hazard.
- The dryer vent terminates in the crawl space rather than to the exterior.
